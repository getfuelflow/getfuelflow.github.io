Built around your schedule

Most nutrition apps are made for weight loss. FuelFlow is made for performance. Tell it your sport, how often you train, and when you compete, and it sets daily calorie and macro targets to match. Add a tournament, game, or race, and FuelFlow helps you fuel for the days leading up to it.

How it works

  1. Tell FuelFlow about you: your sport, training, goals, and food preferences.
  2. Get your targets: daily calories, protein, carbs, and fat built for how you train.
  3. Hit them every day: with meal plans, quick food logging, and a coach you can ask anything.
