Frequently asked questions

What's free vs Premium?

Free includes food logging and search, personalized calorie and macro targets, weight and sleep tracking, and progress charts. Premium adds AI meal plans and recipes, photo food recognition, restaurant and menu suggestions, the AI nutrition coach, and adaptive calorie suggestions.

How does the free trial work?

Premium Monthly and Premium Yearly both include a 1-week free trial for new subscribers, so you can try the AI features first. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your subscription starts automatically at the plan price.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then FuelFlow. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

Is FuelFlow medical advice?

No. FuelFlow provides general nutrition information for healthy athletes. It isn't a substitute for advice from a doctor or registered dietitian.

Who is FuelFlow for?

Athletes in every sport, ages 13 and older, on iPhone and iPad.
