How Much Protein Do Athletes Need?

Protein helps your muscles repair and adapt after training, so athletes need more of it than people who aren't active. But more isn't always better, and most athletes can get enough from normal meals. Here's how much you need and how to get it.

The general range

Sports nutrition experts commonly recommend that athletes eat about 1.2 to 2.0 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day. That's roughly 0.5 to 0.9 grams per pound. Athletes doing a lot of hard training or strength work tend to be toward the higher end.

You don't need to calculate this exactly. If you have a good protein source at every meal and some snacks, most athletes land in the right range.

Spread it out

Your body uses protein best in moderate amounts spread across the day, rather than one huge serving at dinner. A simple target: protein at every meal, plus a protein-containing snack after training.

Easy visual: a portion of meat, fish, or tofu about the size of your palm is a good amount for one meal.

Good protein sources

  • Animal: chicken, turkey, beef, fish, eggs, milk, Greek yogurt, cheese
  • Plant: beans, lentils, chickpeas, tofu, tempeh, edamame, peanut butter, nuts, soy milk
  • Easy snacks: chocolate milk, yogurt, string cheese, hard-boiled eggs, a turkey sandwich

What a day might look like

  • Breakfast: eggs with toast, or yogurt with granola
  • Lunch: chicken or turkey sandwich, or a rice bowl with beans
  • After training: chocolate milk or Greek yogurt
  • Dinner: fish, chicken, beef, or tofu with rice or pasta and vegetables

Do you need protein shakes?

Usually not. Shakes are just a convenient form of protein. They can help if you're short on time, but they don't build muscle better than real food. Teens should check with a doctor before using protein powders or other supplements.

Don't forget carbs

Protein gets the attention, but carbohydrates are your main fuel for training and games. An athlete who eats lots of protein but not enough carbs will often feel tired and recover slowly.
