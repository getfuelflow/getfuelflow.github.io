What's free and what's Premium?

Free: food logging and food search, personalized calorie and macro targets, weight and sleep tracking, and progress charts.

Premium: AI meal plans and recipes, photo food recognition, restaurant and menu suggestions, the AI nutrition coach, and adaptive calorie suggestions.

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is $9.99 per month or $79.99 per year (US prices). Both plans include a 1-week free trial for new subscribers.

How does the free trial work?

When you start a trial, you get full Premium access for one week at no cost. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your subscription starts automatically and you'll be charged the plan price through your Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → FuelFlow, then tap Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for.

I already subscribed but Premium is locked. What do I do?

  • Make sure you're signed in to the same FuelFlow account you used when you subscribed.
  • Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases.
  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Still stuck? Email us with the email address on your FuelFlow account and we'll fix it.

How do I get a refund?

All payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your data. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

Important: Deleting your FuelFlow account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone's Settings as well.

Is my data private?

Yes. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

Who can use FuelFlow?

FuelFlow is for users 13 and older and works on iPhone and iPad.

Is FuelFlow's advice medical advice?

No. FuelFlow's targets, meal plans, and AI coach provide general nutrition information for healthy athletes. They aren't a substitute for advice from a doctor or registered dietitian, especially if you have allergies, a medical condition, or specific dietary needs.
