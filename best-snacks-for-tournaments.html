Best Snacks for Tournaments and Game Days

Tournaments, meets, and long game days are won or lost by what's in your bag. Snack stands are full of candy, chips, and soda, so packing your own food is the easiest way to stay fueled from the first game to the last.

Quick energy (less than an hour before you play)

Easy-to-digest carbs that won't sit heavy in your stomach:

  • Bananas
  • Orange slices or grapes
  • Applesauce pouches
  • Pretzels or crackers
  • Rice cakes with honey
  • Dried fruit, like raisins or mango
  • Sports drink

Recovery snacks (right after you play)

Carbs plus protein to refill your energy and help your muscles recover:

  • Chocolate milk
  • Greek yogurt with fruit or granola
  • A turkey, chicken, or peanut butter sandwich
  • String cheese with crackers
  • Trail mix (if you have time before the next game)
  • Hard-boiled eggs with a bagel

Mini meals (2 or more hours between games)

  • Sandwiches or wraps
  • Pasta salad
  • Rice with chicken in a container
  • Bagels with peanut butter and a banana

Your cooler checklist

  • Water bottles (more than you think you'll need)
  • Sports drinks for hot days or long tournaments
  • Ice packs to keep yogurt, milk, and sandwiches cold
  • A mix of quick-energy snacks and recovery snacks
  • Wet wipes, napkins, and a trash bag
Tip: Pack snacks you've already eaten before games. Tournament day isn't the time to find out a new bar doesn't agree with your stomach.

What to skip

  • Greasy or fried foods, like chips, fries, and nachos, right before playing
  • Candy and soda as your main fuel. They give a quick spike, then a crash.
  • Energy drinks
  • Lots of high-fiber foods, like beans or big salads, close to game time
