Swim Meet Nutrition

Swim meets are long days with short, intense races spread out over hours. You might swim a 50 in the morning and a 400 in the afternoon, with a lot of waiting in between. The key is steady, small amounts of fuel all day instead of one big meal.

Before warm-up

Meets often start early. Eat breakfast at least 1.5 to 2 hours before warm-up, focused on carbs with a little protein:

  • Oatmeal with banana and a glass of milk
  • A bagel or toast with peanut butter and fruit
  • Yogurt with granola and berries
  • Eggs with toast
Tip: Very early warm-up? Eat a bigger dinner the night before and have a small, easy breakfast, like a banana and a granola bar.

Between events: graze, don't feast

Pack small snacks and eat a little after each race, depending on how long until your next one:

  • Less than 30 minutes: sips of water or a sports drink only.
  • 30 to 60 minutes: easy carbs, like a banana, applesauce pouch, pretzels, or a few crackers.
  • 1 to 2 hours: a small snack with carbs and a bit of protein, like half a sandwich, yogurt, or a granola bar with milk.
  • 2+ hours (long session break): a light meal, like a sandwich or wrap with fruit.

Yes, swimmers get dehydrated

It's easy to forget you're sweating when you're in the water, but you are, especially on a warm pool deck. Keep a water bottle with you all day and sip between every event. A sports drink helps during long meet days.

Pack a meet bag

  • Water bottle and a sports drink
  • Bananas, grapes, or orange slices
  • Pretzels, crackers, or rice cakes
  • Granola bars
  • Sandwiches or wraps for longer breaks
  • Yogurt or chocolate milk in a small cooler

After the meet

Have a real meal with carbs and protein within about 1 to 2 hours, like pasta, a rice bowl with chicken, or a sandwich with milk. If it's a two-day meet, a good dinner, plenty of water, and a full night of sleep set you up for day two. More ideas: What to Eat After a Workout.

What to avoid

  • A big, heavy meal right before your races
  • Living on candy from the concession stand all day
  • Energy drinks with lots of caffeine
  • Trying new foods on meet day
