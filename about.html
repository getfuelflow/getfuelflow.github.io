About FuelFlow

Sports nutrition built for athletes, by someone who gets it.

Why I built FuelFlow

I started playing tennis when I was young, and as I moved up to more competitive levels, I realized I didn't really know what to eat. I'd run out of energy during long practices and matches, and I never felt confident that I was fueling my body the right way.

I tried a lot of nutrition apps. Each one solved part of the problem: one tracked calories, another had recipes, another gave general tips. But none of them were built for athletes, and none understood what a tournament weekend, a double practice day, or an early-morning match actually takes.

So I decided to build my own. FuelFlow started as a fun challenge, and it turned into something bigger: an app that helps young athletes fuel their bodies with confidence, without stressing over every bite.

Even though my story started on the tennis court, FuelFlow isn't just for tennis players. It's built for athletes in every sport, from soccer and basketball to swimming, running, and beyond. I'm going to keep adding more sports, guides, and features so that every athlete can find what they need here.

I built FuelFlow so other athletes don't have to figure it out the hard way like I did.

What FuelFlow stands for

  • Fueling for performance, not dieting. FuelFlow helps you eat enough to train, compete, and recover well. It isn't a weight-loss app.
  • Built for athletes. Targets and plans are shaped around your sport, your training, and your competition schedule.
  • Honest about AI. FuelFlow's AI gives general information, not medical advice. It doesn't replace a doctor or registered dietitian.
